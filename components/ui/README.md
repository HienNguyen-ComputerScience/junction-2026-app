# FirstLine design system (short)

**Direction.** Sober, exact, plain-spoken: a statistics office's annual report, not a startup page. One dark theme, no toggle. The chrome is near-black and neutral, with one accent that is the product's identity; the only other saturated colour on any screen is data status. The richness goes into the figures and the bars.

**One way to do each thing.** A step, a bar, a button, a link, a caption and a panel title each have one component or one rule below. Before adding a variant, remove a use.

## The pathway steps

`components/pathway/pathway-steps.tsx` is the only component for the three steps, on every page: `buildPathwaySteps(countyId)` reads them from the snapshot, `<PathwaySteps steps density />` draws them.

- Every step, at both densities and on every page, has the same parts in the same order (the steps share subgrid rows): title (mark and title, top-aligned; one- and two-line titles take the same height) · the value alone on its row, so the three figures share one baseline · the comparison in words under it ("30.7 percentage points slower than Finland", "points" in the compact strip; for a hospital group's value, the one line "HUS-wide value, not a county figure"; for step 2, "Not measured anywhere in Finland") · one description line (what the value counts) · the bar with "Finland xx.x%" under its tick · one row with the status chip and the source chip inline · a link on its own line under that row for a step that has one (step 2: "Red-flag helper"). A step without a figure shows a muted dash, the data's own sentence, the hatched bar and its "No public data" chip.
- "No county figure" is the chip of a step that shows a hospital group's value (HUS): it is said once on the card, in the one line beside the value. "HUS-wide" is explained once per page, in the ranking footer.
- Two densities, one layout. `density="full"` (county page): 36px value, three divided columns. `density="compact"` (act page, home): 28px value, a thin bar, step 2 with the short line "No public statistic measures this step", and the status chip, source chip and a step's link on one row (five subgrid rows, about 180px tall), so Action 1's title, First step and evidence headline stay above y=800 at 1280×800.
- The section title gives the year ("… (2025)"), so the chip in a step leaves it out and the status row stays one row. The direction ("lower is better") is not repeated on the card; the red ink and the bar carry it.
- Every step with a value leads with its share (85.4%, 74.1%) as the big figure; the gap to Finland sits beside it. There is no "show the gap instead" variant.
- `TrendStrip` (`components/pathway/trend-strip.tsx`) is the panel under the full steps: per step a figure block (the change since 2023 in words) in a fixed 240px column and the chart filling the rest (176px plot). Only the latest year has a status, so it alone is drawn by the bar rule (upright); the earlier years are neutral grey, and the label strip says "Earlier years in grey for comparison" once. The panel's footer row holds the page's one `BarKey` and the context-only "Referrals waiting over 21 days" line (`footer` prop). Finland's tick on each year is labelled once, under that year on the axis ("Finland 48.5%"), never between the bars. The source chips sit in the strip's right slot, not under the chart. `WorseColumn`, `TREND_FILL` and `TREND_PAST_FILL` are exported for the early-warning rows' small columns.
- `PathwayStrip` (`components/pathway/pathway-strip.tsx`) places the three steps in the eight-step pathway as one stepper: nodes on a line (across from `md`, down on a phone), FirstLine's steps with the step cards' own `StepMark` (number and status) and their status in words under the label, the others small outline dots. Groups are brackets over the line ("Patient", "Where a county can act", "Hospital"), not tags on each node.

## Bars: the Finland line

Every bar is zero-based (0 to 100%) and carries a thin off-white marker at Finland's value. Use `components/ui/measure-bar.tsx`, never a hand-made bar:

- `<MeasureBar value finland finlandLabel status />`: bar, line and axis. `value={null}` draws a hatched empty track (no public data), `finland={null}` draws no line. `size`: `"sm"` 6px (compact steps), default, `"lg"` (the home card). `gapLabel`: the distance to Finland in words ("13.1 points slower than Finland", from `gapInWords` in `components/ui/gap-words.ts`).
- `BarFill`, `FinlandTick`, `FinlandAxis`: the parts, for custom layouts (the ranking draws one continuous 1px line down the bar rows, and only through bar rows).
- **One treatment for "worse than Finland", on every bar (big bars, the ranking's mini bars, the home card, the judged column of the trend):** a wait bar's solid red is the gap to Finland and touches the line; a referral bar ends at its value and its shortfall is hatched, never solid.
  - a **wait** share (lower is better): tinted red up to the Finland line, solid red beyond it;
  - a **referral** share (higher is faster): the tinted fill ends at the value, so the bar visibly ends there; the shortfall from the value up to the Finland line is faded red hatching (`bg-shortfall`, on the track) that does not read as fill.
  - The tint is the opaque token `bg-status-red-tint`. The kind is read from the data; pass `higherIsBetter` only to override.
- At or better than Finland: slate (`bg-status-amber-bar`; the token name is historical). Slate is not "good", and a slate figure stays in the text colour (`STATUS_FIGURE`).
- Grey fill: only a mark without a status, i.e. the "Finland overall" bar and the earlier years of the trend. Hatched track: no public data.
- The map of Finland (`components/map/finland-map.tsx`) fills its areas with the same tokens at the same strength as the bars: `status-red`, `status-amber-bar`, the hatch.
- `<BarKey items />`: the key, one row of swatches drawn like the marks: "Finland's value", "Worse than Finland's level" (tint then solid), "Gap to Finland's level" (tint then faded hatching: a referral bar short of the line; a mark, not a status word), "At or better than Finland's level", "No public data". **One key per page** (home: under the map; county: under the steps; act: beside the strip's title), with `items` limited to the marks in view. No prose legends: a sentence under a chart is only for what a swatch cannot say.
- `withFigures("Finland 54.7%")` sets the numbers of a one-string label in the figure face.

## Buttons and links

- **Primary**: `Button` (default), the filled accent. At most one per viewport: the main action of the view ("See all actions", "Ask"). Disabled, it is a flat neutral block with faint text: clearly switched off.
- **Secondary**: `Button variant="outline"`, a neutral outline on the raised surface ("Rewrite", "Show 4 more", "Find more evidence live").
- **Tertiary**: a text link. `Button` has no other variants; sizes are default (36px) and `lg` (44px).
- **Links**: `TextLink` (`text-link.tsx`) is the one text link: accent text, no underline until hover, and an icon that says where it goes. `kind="jump"` (further down this page, arrow down), `kind="nav"` (another page of the app, arrow right), `kind="external"` (another site, new tab, the small up-right arrow the source chips use). `inline` sets it in running text. No other link style, and no underlined links.
- `BackLink`: the only back-link pattern (arrow + text, starts on the page edge). Same names for the same destinations everywhere: "Back to all areas" (to `/`), "Back to <area>" (to the county page; the act page has both), and "See all actions" for the button to the act page.
- `Disclosure`: styled `<details>` with a chevron, never a bare `<details>`. Disclosures in one stack share one left edge, and a secondary button under them starts on the same edge (see the action card).
- Rows that navigate (the ranking): the whole row is the link, with a 16px chevron at its right end, a `bg-accent` hover and an inset focus ring.

## Text

Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, Geist for text and numbers, Geist Mono for source chips; weights 600 or below. Sentence case; short labels end without a period.

- `type-display` home h1 · `type-page` page h1 · `type-title` section title (22px; `FrameTitle` applies it) · `type-heading` item title inside a section (17px). An item title never uses `type-title`.
- `type-lede` (17px) · body `text-sm` / `text-[0.9375rem]`.
- **Captions: `type-caption`, 13px, in `text-muted-foreground`.** This is the one size for secondary text: descriptions, row sub-lines, footers, "Who" lines, the quiet status line. There is no smaller prose. A caption says something the screen does not already show; a sentence that restates a label, a chart or a badge is deleted, not shrunk. Limitations, source lines, exact labels and anything read from the data are never cut.
- 12px and 11px are for marks only: `type-note` (labels drawn on a chart or an axis), `type-mark` (mono source chips), badges.
- `type-legend`: 13px, for the labels of `BarKey` (it sets it itself).
- **Numbers: one face, Geist with tabular figures.** `numeral` for standalone key figures, with a size (`numeral text-[2.25rem]`), coloured with `STATUS_FIGURE[status]` (red ink only when worse than Finland). `figure` for a number inside a line of text; wrap the number with its unit and nothing else.
- Say "percentage points" once per section where gaps first appear (a heading line or description); rows say "points", never "pts". Comparisons are words, never a sign: "13.1 points slower than Finland", "1.8 points faster than Finland", one decimal (`gapInWords` in `gap-words.ts`, which reads the direction: a referral share below Finland's is slower). Spell out "HUS (Helsinki University Hospital region)" at its first use on a page (`explainHus`, display only); the step cards keep the label "HUS-wide value, not a county figure". The status words are exactly "Worse than Finland's level", "At or better than Finland's level" and "No public data", on every measure. Change over time is written in words, with no arrow and no colour: "27.4 points worse since 2023".
- Always "non-urgent GP visits"; never people or patients "waiting", and the app never does a "diagnosis".

## Surfaces and frames

- **Two surface levels, never more.** A section is its heading on the page plus its panels: the page (`bg-background` `#050506`) → the panel (`bg-card` `#18191b`, the one raised surface with a hairline) → inside a panel only blocks that mean something: the First step box, inputs, chips (`bg-muted` `#202123`; hovered `bg-accent` `#292b2d`). Items inside a panel are separated by space and 1px hairline dividers, never by another bordered card. `bg-frame` `#0f1011` is left only for the phone limitations disclosure. Nothing may read as navy.
- Room for text: panel padding 20px on a phone and 24px from `sm`; body text at line-height about 1.5 and at most ~68ch; a label sits 4px or more above its value. Make room by removing chrome, never by shrinking type. Prefer one wide card with two inner columns over two narrow cards with wrapped short lines.
- Hairlines carry the edges: `border-frame-border` (10%), `border-border` (12%), `border-input` (14%), the text colour at low opacity. No shadows.
- `Frame` / `FrameHeader` / `FrameTitle` / `FrameMeta` / `FrameDescription` / `FramePanel` / `FrameFooter` (`frame.tsx`): the section. `Frame` itself is no surface (no border, fill or padding); its header and footer sit on the page, flush with the panels' edges. `FrameMeta` sits beside the title from `sm` and under it on a phone. Sections are 40px apart (56px from `sm`).
- **A panel title only where the panel needs one**: `FramePanelLabel` as the first child of a `FramePanel padding="none"`, title on the left, its chip or its one link on the right. No hairline under it by default; `divider` adds one only where rows with their own dividers follow (the brief's rows). A panel without a title has none.
- Text, three roles, all AA on every surface: `text-foreground` `#eaebed`, `text-muted-foreground` `#abaeb2`, `text-faint` `#999c9f` (placeholders, axis ticks, rank numbers, chevrons). Never lighter than `text-faint`.
- **Accent**: one colour, a cold glacier cyan, `brand` `#67cfe3`. Used for the logo mark, the active nav underline, the primary button, text links (`text-brand-ink`), the focus ring, the selected red-flag rows (1px border, faint tint) and the 3px strip of the "First step" box. Nothing else.
- Status (data only): `status-red` (worse than Finland, the only alarm colour), `status-amber` (slate; at or better than Finland's level), `status-grey` (no public data, hatched). Each has `-soft`, `-border`, `-ink`. Notes, banners, results and errors are neutral.
- Radius: `rounded-frame` 20px → `rounded-panel` 14px → `rounded-lg` 10px (buttons, inputs, inner blocks) → `rounded-sm` 6px (chips, badges). `rounded-full` only for the step dots.
- Spacing: 4px grid. Container: `page-container`. Sections: 40px apart, 56px from `sm` (the act page 32px, so Action 1's First step stays on its first screen). Panel padding: `px-5 sm:px-6`.
- Dark by default, with no `.dark` class: `:root` holds the dark tokens. `@custom-variant dark (&:is(.dark *))` stays in `globals.css` to keep every `dark:` class inert. Do not add a `dark` class or `dark:` variants.

## Tags

- `StatusBadge`: an 8px square mark plus a text label, never colour alone. `variant="chip"` (tag; `size="xs"` is as tall as a source chip), `"plain"` (coloured text), `"quiet"` (the mark plus a 13px caption, for dense rows). Take status from the data. In a list, the group heading carries the status label once, with its count and its source chips.
- `SourceChip` (`components/source-chip.tsx`): mono tag with an arrow. Every number has one, or a source line, near it. One format: "Sotkanet 6411" or, where no title gives the year, "Sotkanet 6411 · 2025"; evidence sources show the publisher's name.
- **The 8px square is for data status only.** Provenance is a 14px lucide icon plus 13px muted text, no box: `<Badge variant="unverified">Found live — unverified</Badge>` (`Globe`), `<Badge variant="checked">Source checked</Badge>` (`ShieldCheck`) and `<Badge variant="checked" source="snapshot">From the published figures</Badge>` (`Database`). Same muted colour, icon in the text colour.
- Live content (`components/exa/result-list.tsx`): `LiveMark` is the one provenance and freshness line, in the frame header: the unverified badge, a middle dot and "Live web search via Exa", all on one line; or "Web search via Exa · cached · saved 2026-11-14". Rows are two columns (`grid-cols-[1fr_auto]`): the title (link, two lines), the snippet if any (two lines), a caption "host · 12 Nov 2026" (absolute dates, ISO in the `title`; never "3 days ago"), and the external arrow top-aligned in the right column. A row without a snippet is the title and the caption.
- Icons: lucide only, 16px (the up-right arrow of an external link or a chip is 14px), stroke 1.75, in the colour of the text beside them.

## Pages

- Nav (`components/site-nav.tsx`): "Areas" (`/` and `/county/<id>`), "Actions" (the act page of the area in `?area=` on home, of the current county, else of the area that leads the ranking), "Ask the evidence" (scrolls to the ask box; never active), "Sources & limitations". Active state by route.

- Page pattern: `BackLink`, a one-line context caption, `type-page` h1, then Frames.
- Act: the compact steps, then Action 1 with its "First step" box inside the first 1280×800 screen.
- County: the limitations box (title and content) is inside the first 1280×800 screen, never clipped. The hero reads name → key figure (`numeral`, 76px, badge and chip stacked beside it) → its caption → the verdict's first sentence (`type-lede`, two lines at most) → the verdict's other sentences as one muted `text-sm` line, each source chip right after the sentence it sources (Sotkanet 13 after the HUS figure). The verdict's words come from `lib/` unchanged; only where they are shown is split. On a phone the limitations disclosure reads "Limitations (8): read before acting".
- Action cards (act page and the county brief) use two tones: the action title in `text-foreground`; every other line (description, Owner, Precedent, Evidence, Who, labels) in `text-muted-foreground`, labels in `type-caption`. The left column reads title, First step box, description, Owner, so the First step is on the first 1280×800 screen of the act page. Only the "First step" box changes surface (`bg-muted`, flat, with a 3px accent strip on its left). The action title is the largest text of the card (26px). The evidence column starts with the "Evidence" label directly above its content: a stat in plain words (24px `numeral`: "25% lower odds of dying", "Diagnosis 17 days faster", "97.6% judged safe"; built by `plainStat` in `action-card.tsx` only from `effect_size` value, unit and metric when they state the direction outright, otherwise no stat), then the Evidence text in `text-foreground`, then the precedent as one line clamped to two (the full text behind a "Full precedent" disclosure when it is long), then the one disclosure "Evidence details" (full precedent, the original statistic with the quote and page in the source, and the limitations). Action titles are shown without a trailing full stop (`plainTitle`; the data keeps its text). No `text-faint` in body lines.
- The red-flag helper before any input is one `FramePanel` with a label strip ("Warning-sign category"), a 16px icon, "No concern picked yet" and one caption. The result rows (how fast, what the GP does, the guideline links) appear only with a result; the result column is sticky (`top-24`, self-start) so it never leaves a blank area. A guideline link shows its name as the link and the detail after the first colon in muted text; an inline `TextLink` keeps its last word and icon together.
- A recommended action in the county brief is one row (title with its source chip on the title row and "Owner" under it, and the First step block, the same component as everywhere); the section has one link, "See the evidence and limitations", to the act page.
- Home: the first screen is one composed block. Left: the h1 (40px, two lines: "In Finland, your area decides how long a non-urgent GP visit waits") with a 16px muted sub-line that says what to do (no figure in it, so the selected area's figure is the one number of the first screen), then one card: the area picker as its title row ("Choose your area", "or click an area on the map", "Compare all 22 areas"), the selected area's key figure (`numeral`, 76px, `STATUS_FIGURE`) with its status badge and source chip stacked beside it and one caption, the verdict (`type-heading`), "See all actions" with "Full pathway for …" beside it, and under a hairline the top action (action and owner | First step box and evidence chip). Right: the map card, its map filling the block's height (title and chips, the map, the credit line, the key). Under the block: the what-if card, then the compact three steps.
- What-if (`components/home/what-if-panel.tsx`): drawn, not told. The headline is the sentence ("30.7 fewer of every 100 non-urgent GP visits would wait over 7 days"). The area's bar (`BarFill`, so the solid red is already the gap to Finland), every mark labelled in 12px on one baseline under the bar, in the order the marks sit, each led by its mark's glyph ("Best: Päijät-Häme 32.7%", "Finland 54.7%", "Now 85.4%"), a bracket over the span the arithmetic counts and the best area as a dotted mark. the caption "Arithmetic on the published shares, not a forecast of any single action." stays.
- Early warning (`components/home/early-warning.tsx`): per row the change in words as the 15px key figure, the latest value with its status chip, and a 220px chart of three zero-based columns (2023 to 2025) with each value printed above its bar, earlier years grey, the latest by the bar rule, Finland's latest value as the line across it (named once under the heading).
- Map in picker mode: the selected area has a 3px accent outline over a 6px panel-coloured under-stroke, drawn on top of its neighbours, and a lighter fill, and the others step back to 72% opacity (full on hover or focus). The Helsinki region is enlarged in an inset in the map's empty north-east corner (`inset`), with a dashed box on the main map, so Helsinki and Vantaa and Kerava can be clicked. Åland is not in the data and is not drawn.
- The area picker invites a choice in plain words ("Choose your area", "Or click an area on the map"); buttons and links say where they go ("See all actions", "Full pathway for …", "Compare all 22 areas"). The hero card holds the primary button under the verdict, so the way to the actions is on the first screen. A step without data points to the next useful thing (step 2 links to the red-flag helper on home and act). The compact steps put the number under the title on shared rows (subgrid). "Owner" is a label/value pair everywhere (muted label, value beside it; on the home card the label sits above the value). The UI says "step", never "stage".
- Phone (390px): frames have no border or padding, so cards sit at the page gutter, and the "See all actions" button is full width; one-row 56px header, stacked columns, tap targets 36px or more, no horizontal scroll, a source line directly under the figures it sources.

**Motion**: `animate-bar-grow` (bars, staggered with `--i`) and `animate-fade-up`, one curve, under 0.6s, off with `prefers-reduced-motion`. Hover is a surface step or a text-colour change only.

**Don't**: gradients, shadows, glows, pills, all-caps tracked labels, the accent outside its uses, a second accent, green, text lighter than `text-faint`, prose under 13px, a second key on a page, a prose legend, a second filled button in a viewport, an underlined link, a status label repeated on every row, a number twice in one viewport, truncating meaningful text, a `SourceChip` nested inside another link, a red bar whose solid part does not touch the Finland line, or a bar without the Finland line when Finland's value exists.
