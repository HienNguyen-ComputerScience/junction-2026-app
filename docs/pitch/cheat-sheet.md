# Pitch cheat sheet (keep this on your phone)

## The only 3 things to remember

1. **Every number is from THL** (Finland's health institute). Click the grey button next to any number to see the source.
2. **Grey means nobody publishes that data.** We never guess.
3. **The app tells a county where its delay is, and what to do about it.**

## Three numbers

| | Non-urgent GP visits that wait over a week (2025) |
|---|---|
| Vantaa and Kerava | **85%** (almost 9 in 10) |
| All of Finland | **55%** (about half) |
| Päijät-Häme | **33%** (a third) |

## If a judge asks…

| They ask | You say | You click |
|---|---|---|
| "Where does this number come from?" | "THL's official national statistics. Here's the source." | The grey button next to the number |
| "How do you know Finland is 54.7%?" | "THL publishes it. We just show it." | The grey button next to "Finland 54.7%" |
| "Is this about cancer?" | "It's all GP visits. It's the best public measure of getting in to a GP, and the app says so." | (none) |
| "Why is step 2 grey?" | "Finland doesn't measure how well GPs spot cancer. England does. That's a gap Finland should fix." | (none) |
| "Why is step 3 grey for Vantaa?" | "Their hospital group, HUS, only reports one number for all its areas together. We don't copy it onto each area." | (none) |
| "Did these actions really work?" | "Each one shows the honest result and its source." | An action card |
| "Something you don't know" | "Good question. It's on our Sources & limitations page." | **Sources & limitations** in the top menu |

## If you get stuck

Say: **"Every number is sourced. Let me show you,"** and click a grey button.
